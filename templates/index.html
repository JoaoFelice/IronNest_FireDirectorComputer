Quick reference for the Iron Nest Ballistics console.

✛ Calculating a shot

  1. 123 Tap DISTANCE to activate it, then type the range in km on the keypad. You don't need the . key for whole numbers — type 1234 to get 12.34, or type . yourself for something like 7.5.
  2. 3 (Optional) Tap a PROPELLANT CHARGE button (1–6). Skip it and the console auto-picks the smallest charge that reaches your distance the moment you calculate.
  3. ° (Optional) Tap DIRECTION and type the bearing (0–360°, up to 1 decimal). Leave it empty if you don't need it.
  4. CALC Tap CALCULATE. The shot is added to the FIRE LOG, and DISTANCE/DIRECTION clear automatically — the charge stays selected for your next shot.
  5. ⚠ If the input is invalid (e.g. distance beyond the charge's range), an error appears above the buttons and nothing clears, so you can fix it and try again.

↓ How the lists are ordered

  • New shots are added to the bottom of the active list — it reads top to bottom in the order you calculated them, like a queue.
  • The big NEXT ELEVATION readout always shows the topmost card of the active list — the next shot in line — together with its rotation. It isn't always the last one you calculated.
  • Marking a shot fired moves it out of the active list into its own FIRED section, which always sits below the active list, in its own scroll box.
  • Each shot also shows a TOF (time of flight) estimate — how many seconds the shell is in the air before impact. It's interpolated from in-game readings taken at each charge and elevation, not a formula.

✓ Marking fired & deleting

  • ✓ Tap the check to mark a shot as fired — it turns grey and drops into the FIRED section. Tap it again on a fired card to undo — it returns to the active list. This button never disappears, so you can always toggle it back.
  • ✕ Tap the cross to permanently delete that entry from the log. This can't be undone.

⚙ Other controls

  • CLEAR resets DISTANCE and DIRECTION only — it never touches the charge selection or the log.
  • Tap the already-selected charge again to deselect it and return to automatic mode.
  • FDC / IRONCLAD at the top switches the console's look — your shots and settings are identical in both.
  • Everything is saved on this device automatically; nothing is sent anywhere else.
